# Project Context & Vision

## Goal Sync Portal (Syncora)

Syncora is an enterprise goal-setting and telemetry portal engineered with a premium cinematic graphite aesthetic. It discards generic SaaS dashboard templates in favor of a sophisticated visual identity featuring layered graphite depth, typography-driven hierarchy, calm spring-based motion architecture, and intentional spatial pacing.

## Core Objectives

1. **High-Performance Telemetry**: Provide real-time goal tracking, metrics, and alignment visualization across enterprise teams.
2. **Cinematic Editorial UX/UI**: Deliver a stunning, non-templated experience using rich dark modes, glassmorphism, and curated typography (Geist).
3. **Flawless 3D Asset Integration**: Seamlessly embed interactive Spline 3D scenes (Hero and Auth) with robust runtime guards and fallback states.
4. **Resilient Architecture**: Maintain clean separation of concerns between Next.js frontend routes, state management (Zustand), and backend services (Prisma/Supabase).
